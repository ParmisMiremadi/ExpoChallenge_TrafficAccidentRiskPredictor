# 🚦 Traffic Accident Risk Predictor

### AI Challenge 6 – Innoverse 2026

An AI-powered system that predicts traffic accident risk levels and identifies dangerous road zones before accidents occur.

---

## 📌 Overview

Traffic accidents are influenced by many factors including weather conditions, road characteristics, location, and time-related patterns.

This project uses machine learning to analyze historical accident data and predict future accident risk levels, helping enable proactive traffic safety management.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Predict traffic accident risk levels using machine learning.
- Identify high-risk geographical areas.
- Analyze seasonal and environmental effects.
- Reduce false alarms while maintaining reliable predictions.
